# Production Extension Strategies

## Goal: Year-Round Indoor Berry Production

## Strategy 1: Day-Neutral Varieties
- Day-neutral strawberries (Seascape, Albion) produce continuously regardless of day length
- No dormancy period needed
- Under controlled indoor lighting, they fruit 12 months/year
- This is the foundation of the system

## Strategy 2: Variety Staggering
- Plant varieties with different ripening times
- Early, mid, and late season varieties ensure something is always producing
- Example: Albion (early) + Seascape (mid) + Evie 2 (continuous)

## Strategy 3: Successive Planting
- Start new plants every 2-3 weeks
- As older plants slow down, younger ones come into production
- Runners from existing plants = free successive plantings
- Especially useful for determinate tomato companions (Micro Tom, Tiny Tim)

## Strategy 4: Managed Dormancy Cycling (Raspberries)
- Primocane raspberries fruit on first-year canes
- After harvest, force 4-6 week cold period (fridge/cold garage)
- Return to tent -> new canes -> second harvest
- Achieve 2-3 harvest cycles per year vs 1 outdoors
- Philips research validates this approach

## Strategy 5: Multi-Tent Species Separation
- Each species in its own tent with optimized conditions
- Tent 1: Strawberries (no dormancy, continuous)
- Tent 2: Blackberries (trained on trellis, annual cycle)
- Tent 3: Raspberries (managed dormancy cycles)
- Windowsill: Alpine strawberries (zero maintenance)

## Strategy 6: Vertical Growing
- Strawberry towers: fit 90 plants/m2 vs 10-12 in flat arrangement
- Vertical farming techniques from commercial operations
- Stack multiple levels under the same light

## Strategy 7: Light Manipulation
- Extend "days" to 14-16 hours for continuous production
- Run lights during cool nighttime hours (6PM-8AM)
- Dim to 50-60% for strawberries (saves electricity)
- Adjust spectrum: more red during fruiting for sweetness
