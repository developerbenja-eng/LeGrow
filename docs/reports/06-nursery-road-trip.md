# Nursery Road Trip: Boone, NC → Memphis, TN

## Route Overview
Boone, NC → Asheville/Black Mountain → Knoxville → (optional McMinnville detour) → Nashville → Memphis

## Stop-by-Stop Guide

### STOP 1: Asheville / Black Mountain Area (~1.5 hrs from Boone)

**Useful Plants Nursery** — Black Mountain, NC
- Permaculture-based, organic, no synthetic pesticides
- Located at Earthaven Ecovillage, 111 Another Way
- In stock: Raspberries (Heritage, Caroline, Fall Gold) 1-2 gal pots, Blueberries $25-35, Elderberries $25
- Hours: Wed, Thu, Sat 10am-3:30pm. CLOSED Sunday. By appointment any day: (828) 669-6517
- Website: usefulplants.org

**B.B. Barns** — Asheville
- Asheville's largest garden center, 4 acres
- Likely carries Bushel and Berry line
- Hours: Mon-Sat 9am-5pm. CLOSED Sunday
- Phone: (828) 650-7300

**Nurtured by Nature Growers** — Arden, NC
- Specializes in dwarf/semi-dwarf fruit trees for containers
- Blackberry, blueberry, dwarf apple, pear, peach, fig
- Primarily at farmers markets (Saturdays)

### STOP 2: Knoxville (~2.5 hrs from Asheville)

**Riverdale Nursery**
- French Broad River, east of downtown Knoxville
- Perennials, herbs, companion plants
- 3220 Rush Miller Rd, Knoxville, TN 37914
- OPEN SUNDAY: 9am-6pm
- Phone: (865) 546-2829

### STOP 3: McMinnville, TN (~2 hrs from Knoxville) — DETOUR

**"The Nursery Capital of the World"** — 300+ growers in one small town
- ~30 min detour off I-40 near Cookeville
- Best prices on the entire route (wholesale-direct)

Key nurseries:
- Tennessee Wholesale Nursery — sells individual plants at sub-retail prices (tnnursery.net)
- Vaughn Nursery — fruit tree growers since 1959
- Freedom Tree Farms — 550 acres, bareroot and container plants
- Most are Mon-Sat only, CALL AHEAD for retail hours

### STOP 4: Nashville (~1.5 hrs from McMinnville)

**Bates Nursery & Garden Center**
- Nashville tradition since 1932
- Fruit & nut trees, native plants, huge selection
- 3810 Whites Creek Pike, Nashville, TN 37207
- OPEN SUNDAY: 12pm-4pm (starting March 8)
- Phone: (615) 876-1014

**Riverbend Nurseries** — Franklin, TN
- 275 acres, 1500+ varieties, open year-round
- Mon-Fri 8am-4:30pm, Sat 8am-4pm. CLOSED Sunday

### STOP 5: Memphis (Home!)

**Dan West Garden Center**
- Memphis tradition for 70+ years
- 1000+ plant species per season, fruit trees listed
- 4763 Poplar Ave, Memphis, TN 38117
- OPEN SUNDAY: 11am-5pm
- Phone: (901) 767-6743
- Ask for: Bushel & Berry line, dwarf blueberries, alpine strawberry starts, fruit trees

## What to Buy Where
| Plant | Best Stop | Why |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| Raspberry plants | Useful Plants (Black Mountain) | Organic, Heritage/Caroline/Fall Gold in stock |
| Blueberry plants | Useful Plants or B.B. Barns | $25-35, multiple varieties |
| Dwarf fruit trees | Nurtured by Nature (Arden) or McMinnville | Container specialists |
| Bushel & Berry branded | B.B. Barns or Dan West (Memphis) | Check Bushel & Berry store locator |
| Best prices overall | McMinnville TN | Wholesale-direct, save 30-50% |

## Pro Tips
1. Bring a cooler/damp towels — wrap bare roots in wet newspaper
2. Call ahead — especially McMinnville (wholesale hours vary)
3. Use Bushel & Berry store locator: bushelandberry.com/shop
4. McMinnville detour is worth it for the prices
