# Growing Conditions Quick Reference

## Strawberry (Seascape / Day-Neutral) --- Primary Crop

### Environment
| Parameter | Optimal | Acceptable Range | Action if Out |
|-----------|---------|-----------------|---------------|
| Air Temp (day) | 68-75°F | 60-85°F | Fan on >80°F, Alert <55°F |
| Air Temp (night) | 50-54°F | 45-65°F | Alert if out of range |
| Humidity | 40-60% | 30-70% | Fan on >70% (mold risk) |
| Soil Moisture | 50-70% | 40-85% | Water at <40%, stop at 80% |
| Soil pH | 5.8-6.2 | 5.5-6.5 | pH Up/Down adjustment |
| CO2 | 800-1200ppm | 400-2000ppm | Fresh air if <400 |

### Light
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| PPFD | 300-350 umol/m2/s |
| DLI | 17-22 mol/m2/day |
| Photoperiod | 14-16 hours on |
| Spectrum | Full spectrum, 3:1 red:blue |
| Light dimming | 50-60% of 300W fixture |

### Nutrition
| Stage | Fertilizer | Frequency |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Transplant | Light balanced feed | Once at planting |
| Vegetative | Balanced NPK (10-10-10) | Every 2 weeks |
| Flowering | Higher P&K (5-10-10) | Every 2 weeks |
| Fruiting | Higher K (5-5-10) | Every 2 weeks |

### Timeline
| Week | Stage | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| 0 | Transplant bare roots | Soak 1hr, plant crown at soil level |
| 1-4 | Vegetative | Remove early flowers for stronger plant |
| 4-6 | Runners appear | Train or snip for mother plant energy |
| 6-8 | Flowering | Hand-pollinate with brush if needed |
| 8-12 | First fruit | Harvest when fully red |
| 12+ | Continuous | Ongoing harvest, feed regularly |

### Common Problems
| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| No fruit | Not enough light | Increase to 300+ PPFD |
| Small berries | Over-crowding or low nutrients | Thin runners, feed more |
| Mushy/moldy fruit | Humidity too high | Increase airflow, reduce humidity |
| Yellow leaves | Nutrient deficiency or overwatering | Check pH, reduce watering |
| Leggy growth | Not enough light | Lower light or increase intensity |
| No flowers | Too much nitrogen | Switch to bloom fertilizer |

## Raspberry (Heritage / Polka) --- Future Expansion

### Environment
| Parameter | Optimal | Range |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| Air Temp (day) | 65-75°F | 60-80°F |
| Air Temp (night) | 55-60°F | 50-65°F |
| Humidity | 50-70% | 40-75% |
| Soil pH | 5.6-6.2 | 5.5-6.5 |
| Light | 12-16 hours, 400+ PPFD | |
| Container | 18"+ wide, 12"+ deep | |

### Dormancy Management (Indoor)
- After first harvest flush, move to cold location (30-45°F)
- Cold period: 4-6 weeks minimum
- Return to tent -> new canes -> second harvest
- Can achieve 2-3 harvest cycles per year indoors

## Blackberry (Triple Crown / Chester) --- Future Expansion

### Environment
| Parameter | Optimal | Range |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| Air Temp | 70-80°F | 60-85°F |
| Humidity | 45-65% | 40-70% |
| Soil pH | 5.5-6.5 | 5.0-7.0 |
| Light | 14-16 hours, 400+ PPFD | |

### Training (SCROG-style)
- Run 3-4 horizontal wires on wall, 12" apart
- Weave trailing canes along wires flat
- Creates "living fruit wall" in minimal floor space
- Prune fruited canes after harvest, train new ones

## Blueberry (Jelly Bean / Perpetua) --- Future Expansion

### Environment
| Parameter | Optimal | Range |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| Air Temp | 65-75°F | 55-85°F |
| Humidity | 50-60% | 40-70% |
| Soil pH | 4.5-5.0 | 4.0-5.5 (VERY acidic!) |
| Soil mix | Peat moss + pine bark | No lime! |
| Light | 12-16 hours | |

### Notes
- Lifespan: 20-50+ years
- Need acidic soil -- use sulfur or vinegar to lower pH
- Perpetua variety gives TWO crops per year
- Self-pollinating but better with 2 varieties

## Alpine Strawberry (Baron von Solemacher) --- Windowsill Companion

### Environment
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Light | Windowsill or basic LED, 12+ hours |
| Temp | Room temperature (65-75°F) |
| Soil | Standard potting mix |
| Container | 4-6" pot, half gallon |
| Watering | Keep moist, not wet |

### Notes
- Grow from seed ($3-4 per packet, dozens of plants)
- Fruits in 3 months from sowing
- No runners (stays tidy)
- Self-seeds after 3-5 year lifespan
- Day-neutral (fruits continuously)
